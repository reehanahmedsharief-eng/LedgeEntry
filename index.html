Feature checklist

Every tool is available from the home grid and profile settings.

  • Multi-currency with live conversion and offline fallback rates.
  • Lock the app with PIN or pattern, optional face lock, recovery code.
  • Templates for invoices, messages and reminders.
  • Notifications via push, WhatsApp, SMS and email channels.
  • Guide — an in-app assistant named Sam with customizable look and position.
  • Export statements to Excel, CSV and PDF.
  • Audit log of every action for transparency.
  • Dark mode and adjustable text size.
  • Attachments — photos and voice notes on expenses, income and notes.
  • On-device account — no signup, no server, your data stays yours.

Frequently asked questions

Does it need internet?

No. Everything runs on-device. Internet is only used for optional live currency rates and Gemini AI.

Where is my data stored?

In a local SQLite database inside the app’s private storage. It is never uploaded anywhere.

Can I lock the app?

Yes — with a PIN or pattern, and an 8-character recovery code.
